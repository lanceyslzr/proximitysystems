Personal Care

Support with dignity and respect.

Our caregivers assist with daily living: bathing, grooming, dressing, and mobility so clients can feel comfortable and confident every day. We focus on safety, privacy, independence and personal dignity.

Companion Care

Meaningful presence that brightens the day.

We provide social interaction, conversation, light activities, and community engagement to reduce isolation and promote emotional wellbeing. A friendly face can make all the difference.

Learn more about Companion Care

Respite Care

Relief for families, peace for everyone.

Caregiving is rewarding but demanding. Our respite services allow family caregivers to rest, recharge, or attend to personal needs, knowing their loved one is in compassionate, capable hands.

Learn more about Respite Care

Palliative Care

Comfort, compassion, and dignity in every moment.

We provide gentle support for clients and families navigating serious illness or end of life care. Our caregivers focus on comfort, respect, and emotional presence, working alongside healthcare teams when needed.

Learn more about Palliative Care

Dementia Care

Understanding care for unique needs.

Our dementia support emphasizes patience, consistency, and connection. We adapt routines, activities, and communication styles to maintain familiarity and calm, helping clients feel secure at home.

Learn more about Dementia Care

Post Hospital Support

Safe transitions, steady recovery.

Returning home after hospitalization can be overwhelming. We provide assistance with mobility, medication reminders, nutrition, and daily tasks to ensure a smooth, confident recovery at home.

Overnight Care

Reassuring support through the night, when being alone may no longer feel safe.

Personalized overnight support for older adults who may need assistance with nighttime routines, mobility, toileting, personal care, or reassurance throughout the night.

Dedicated care, right where you live.

Care at Home

Home Care That Adapts to Your Routine

Our services are personalized to meet each client’s unique circumstances, whether it’s aging in place, recovering from illness, or adjusting to ongoing health or care changes. We support individuals in remaining at home with confidence, comfort, and independence.

At Daily Home Care, home care is more than a service. It’s a relationship built on trust, consistency, and compassion, guided by close involvement and thoughtful coordination.

We’re proud to serve families across Niagara, from St. Catharines, Niagara Falls, Thorold, Welland and surrounding Niagara communities, providing local care that feels personal, responsive, and close to home.
